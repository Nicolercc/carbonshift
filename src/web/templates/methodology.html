Emissions confidence levels
Measured High confidence
Reported under NYC Local Law 84/97 energy disclosure for buildings ≥25,000 sq ft. These are actual metered GHG totals from the latest filing year.
Class median Medium confidence
No direct filing for this building. CarbonShift applies the median emissions intensity of measured peers with the same PLUTO building class, scaled by floor area.
Borough median Lower confidence
Fewer than three class peers exist. Falls back to a Queens-wide median intensity. Useful for direction, not precision.
Risk labels on the map

Risk scores combine violations (including asbestos flags), building age, and relative emissions intensity. Colors on the map reflect these labels:

  • Low — fewer condition signals
  • Moderate — mixed signals
  • High — elevated concern
  • Critical — strongest retrofit priority
  • Unscored — insufficient inputs

On the map, open any building card to see both the risk headline and emissions confidence before visiting the full building profile.

Data sources

All records come from NYC Open Data: PLUTO building profiles, HPD and DOB violations, asbestos abatement filings (ACP-7), and LL84/97 energy disclosures. Buildings are joined through the Building Footprints BIN↔BBL crosswalk.
